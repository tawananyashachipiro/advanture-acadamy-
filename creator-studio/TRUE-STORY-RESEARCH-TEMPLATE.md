Preferred source order:
1. Primary records from the organisation involved (official mission records, archives, original documents).
2. Museums, universities, public research institutions and peer-reviewed publications.
3. Reputable journalism for context, clearly distinguished from primary evidence.

Do not treat search snippets, social posts, AI summaries or unsourced videos as evidence. If sources disagree, describe the uncertainty rather than choosing a convenient version.

## Storytelling checks
- Separate documented facts from dramatic reconstruction.
- Do not invent dialogue, private thoughts, motives or scenes.
- Label any reenactment or illustrative scene.
- Avoid sensationalising disasters, injuries or deaths.
- Do not imply a single person acted alone when the source documents a team effort.
- Check names, dates, locations, units and pronunciation.
